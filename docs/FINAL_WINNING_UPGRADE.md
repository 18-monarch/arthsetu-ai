## Judge story

2. Open `/model-transparency` and show the real metrics and limitations.
3. Create a new account and complete the questionnaire.
4. Open `/app/improve`, change three habits and run a live re-score.
5. Show the account-specific Neon assessment row.

## Responsible-use position

ArthSetu is an educational financial-readiness prototype. It is not an official
bureau score, lending decision or regulated investment recommendation.
